## 00a_Resonance_Web.py

We study a $(m, n)$ difference resonance. The phase advances per turn $\omega_x, \omega_y$ (the tunes are
$Q = \omega / 2\pi$) satisfy a $(m, n)$ difference resonance condition when

$$
m\,\omega_x - n\,\omega_y = 2\pi k, \qquad m, n \in \mathbb{N},\; k \in \mathbb{Z}.
$$

The order of the resonance is $m + n$.

**00a_Resonance_Web.py** plots the resonance web: every line $m\,\omega_x - n\,\omega_y = 2\pi k$ up to a maximum order, in the $(\omega_x, \omega_y)$ plane, darker for lower orders, together with the working point.
